One useful distinction

Standard, rule, guidance, and evidence are different objects.

W3C publishes WCAG as a technical standard. A rule or contract can reference a version and level. Acquisition guidance helps teams specify and validate work. Actual conformance still depends on evidence from the complete process—not the label alone.
